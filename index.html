About Your Teacher

Meet Hafiza
Binte Waheed

Assalam u Alaikum. I'm Hafiza Binte Waheed, a certified Hafiza and Tajweed instructor passionate about making the Quran simple and accessible for every sister and child.

Through personalised online classes, I've had the honour of teaching 250+ students across 10+ countries, helping them memorise and recite the Quran with confidence and correct Tajweed.

I believe every sister and child deserves to experience the beauty of the Quran without feeling overwhelmed. That's why my vision is to make Hifz and Tajweed simple, structured, and enjoyable, helping students build a lifelong love for the Quran.

For me, teaching the Quran is more than a profession—it's a means of serving the Book of Allah and helping others develop a sincere connection with it. May Allah ﷻ make us among Ahlul Qur'an, the people of the Quran. Ameen. 🤍🤲🏻
